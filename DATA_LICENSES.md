# DATA_LICENSES.md

- No external datasets are used. All keys/values in `core/linear_memory.py`
  are synthetically generated at runtime with numpy's random generator.
- No pretrained weights are used or redistributed. BDH/BDH-CQ weights are
  not included, downloaded, or required.
- No third-party fonts, images, or graphics are used; `web/index.html` uses
  system fonts and hand-drawn Canvas/SVG visuals only.
